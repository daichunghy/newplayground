## Delivered round

Compare two authored views of one coastal scene. Find five changes within 90 seconds; three misses end the round. Tap/click either image, or focus it and move a visible cursor with arrow keys before Enter. Found spots receive matching markers on both pictures. Pause freezes the clock, Restart resets the round, and timeout/miss exhaustion offers replay.

At narrow widths the pictures stack at a readable 5:3 ratio; wider screens place them side by side. Input positions normalize against each image, so resizing keeps the authored targets aligned.
