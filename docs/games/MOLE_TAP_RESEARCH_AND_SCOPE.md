# Đập Chuột Chũi — original candidate

Catalog ID: `dap-chuot-chui`. This P3 discovery item had no registry entry, game module, model, quality record or game-specific sourced art in the local checkout before this batch. The catalog suffix names a known historic game; this work does not claim parity with a particular edition.

## Delivered round

Tap one rising target in a 3×3 garden. Catch twelve to win; three missed targets or wrong holes end the round. Each catch scores 100 points. A target stays up for 1,150 ms, 980 ms after four catches, then 820 ms after eight. A bright ring marks the live target and a shrinking bar shows its remaining window.

The round begins immediately, with no tutorial or sound. Touch/click works on each hole; arrow keys move a roving focus, Enter/Space activate its hole, and number keys 1–9 select directly. Pause freezes simulation time, replay resets score/misses, and hidden-page/blur pauses until the player resumes. Closing the modal removes all listeners and animation frames.
